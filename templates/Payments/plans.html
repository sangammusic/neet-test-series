Questions

How do I pay?

Scan the QR code we show you with any UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm and others). Then send us the UTR from your payment, and a screenshot if you like.

How long does approval take?

We check every payment by hand, so it can take a little while. The moment it's approved, your plan unlocks on its own. You don't need to do anything.

Why is the amount not a round number?

We add a few paise so every payment has its own amount. That's how we recognise yours among many. You pay exactly the amount shown, nothing more.

I paid but nothing unlocked

Open your payment from "My payments" and check its status. If it still says verifying after a while, contact support with your UTR and we'll sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Read our refund policy before you pay.
